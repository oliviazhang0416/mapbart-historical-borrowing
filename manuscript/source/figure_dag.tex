\caption{Directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) for the source-selection mechanisms in Sc1--Sc3. In this schematic, $D$ denotes data source (trial or historical), $Z$ treatment assignment, $Y$ an individual outcome, $X$ the measured predictors relevant to the depicted paths and $U$ unmeasured prognostic variables; participant and group indices are suppressed. Sc1: measured covariates predict the outcome but do not influence source selection. Sc2: measured covariates influence both source selection and outcome. Sc3: unmeasured variables influence source selection and outcome, and generate observed proxies through $U\to X$, with proxy association indexed by $\rho$. Other measured predictors are omitted from the schematic. The $D\to Z$ edge reflects that historical observations are controls, whereas treatment is assigned within the trial. These diagrams illustrate the simulation mechanisms, not an established causal structure for the clinical application.}
\label{fig:dag_scenarios}
