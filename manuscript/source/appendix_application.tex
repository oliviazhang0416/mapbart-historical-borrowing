\section{Application details and supplementary results}\label{sec:F}
\subsection{Analysis extract and harmonized variables}\label{sec:F.1}
The available merged analysis extract has 30 EloKRd and 253 UCMM observations. Starting from 797 UCMM records, the preparation code excludes negative OS/PFS times (790 remain), requires a recorded induction start (303), excludes ASCT on or before induction (254), and excludes E-Rd/Elo-Rd regimens (253). The 48-control subset used in an earlier analysis is not used. The EloKRd analysis uses the available subset of the published study \citep{Derman2022}.

The EloKRd extract supplies times from first treatment (C1D1); the UCMM code constructs times from induction start. OS ends at death or last contact. UCMM PFS ends at the first progression or death, using the recorded first-progression date with a recurrence-date fallback; otherwise it ends at last contact. Zero UCMM PFS times are floored at 0.1 month before logarithmic analysis. EloKRd PFS status 2 (death without prior progression) is recoded as an event. Differences in source-specific endpoint ascertainment may remain despite this harmonization.

Variable selection first retains variables complete in EloKRd, then drops variables with the greatest UCMM missingness until all 253 UCMM records are complete on the retained set. The final numeric model predictors have no missing values. This data-dependent availability rule should be distinguished from a clinically prespecified adjustment set.

\subsection{Analysis and sensitivity configurations}\label{sec:F.2}
All adjusted methods use the same harmonized predictors and standardize control and treated predictions to the 30 EloKRd profiles. KM remains unadjusted. Table S10 reports the hypothetical EloKRd control for adjusted methods and the observed UCMM cohort RMST for KM. Ratios and differences use those respective controls. The primary lrcBART analysis uses $H_f=10$, $H_g=5$, fixed $w=1$ and requested ESS 30. Sensitivities cross $H_f\in\{10,50\}$ with fixed $w\in\{1,0.9\}$. Each of those four settings uses six requested targets: 30, 22.5, 15 and fractions 0.90, 0.50, 0.25 of its calibration ceiling. The target labels 100, 75 and 50 correspond to percentages of the 30-patient trial here; in simulations those labels are absolute counts.

The five comparator settings are KM, AFT-CP, BART-CP and hierarchical AFT at discrepancy-variance prior scales $s_\tau^2=0.05$ and $s_\tau^2=0.5$. Together with 24 lrcBART settings this gives 29 rows per endpoint. The main table reports $s_\tau^2=0.05$; both hierarchical settings remain in the complete appendix table. RMST contrasts use the draw-by-draw calculations in Appendix D, with posterior medians and equal-tailed 95\% intervals.

\subsection{Construction of the joint-subgroup PFS table}\label{sec:F.3}
The table includes only the 22 joint combinations observed in EloKRd, defined by age band, sex, race, Hispanic/Latino status, cytogenetic risk and ASCT. EloKRd and UCMM counts are tabulated separately for those same combinations using the harmonized analysis extract. The displayed counts sum to 30 and 98, respectively; the other 155 UCMM patients belong to combinations not represented in EloKRd and are omitted from the display, but remain in the historical model fit. Every displayed row has a numerical ESS for its empirical trial-subgroup mean. A group represented by one person is retained and has the pointwise special case of the subgroup-mean definition. Exact ages remain in the predictions; other predictors are not set to a common reference.

The preliminary historical model is the primary PFS specification with $H_f=10$. The 4,000 retained joint posterior predictions give the sample variance of each subgroup posterior mean, including cross-profile dependence. Prior-tree calculations use 10,000 trees per group under the combined-covariate candidate rule, and the expectation over spike variance uses 100,000 chi-square draws with $\nu_0=3$ and the globally selected $s_0^2=0.00630957$.

Every group uses the same historical residual-variance reference and scale law. Table 7 evaluates the full-variance expectation-of-ratios definition, rather than the block-based central curve used for scale selection. It uses the preliminary historical fit, not the final outcome fit. Rows are ordered hierarchically by age band, sex, race, Hispanic/Latino status, cytogenetic risk and ASCT. The two observed-count columns describe patients within each combination, whereas ESS uses information from the full historical cohort. ESS is reported to one decimal place. Only PFS appears in this table; OS remains in the outcome tables.

\subsection{Prior ESS calibration and MCMC diagnostics}\label{sec:F.4}
The preliminary application calibration uses 2,000 warm-up and 4,000 retained draws, 2,000 prior trees, 4,000 scale draws and the 141-value scale grid. Table S11(a) reports requested/capped targets, selected scales, the block-based curve and the full-variance ESS evaluated with the estimated prior-tree weight expectation. The latter uses 100,000 deterministic chi-square quantiles for numerical integration. Differences reflect block averaging and numerical integration error; the reciprocal-variance ordering in Appendix B applies with common scale draws.

Each lrcBART outcome fit uses four chains with 2,000 warm-up and 2,000 retained draws per chain. Table S11(b) reports $\widehat R$, bulk ESS and tail ESS for the log RMST ratio. Prior ESS and MCMC ESS have different meanings: the former concerns equivalent reference information about a target mean, whereas the latter concerns simulation efficiency. These scalar diagnostics do not establish exploration of all tree structures.
